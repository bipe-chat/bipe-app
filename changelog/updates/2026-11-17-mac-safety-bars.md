**Mac: account security warnings, and the bars of a blocked or departed person.**
A chat warns when a member's account security changed, and a message held
for that reason can be sent anyway. A blocked person's chat shows the
unblock bar in place of the composer, and a DM whose other person left
shows the invite again bar.
