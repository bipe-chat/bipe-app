**Bipe on Mac keeps personal and work accounts together.** Link another account from your phone and switch from the sidebar. Personal accounts have separate chats and groups; company accounts have a Work overview with today's counts, catalog and people. Search your room names or filter unread conversations. The Mac preview uses the same version and build numbers as the phone apps.
