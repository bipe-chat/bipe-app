**Mac: a person's profile, with block and report.** The info button of a
direct chat and the chat menu open the person's profile: photo, name, @,
seals, facts, bio and social accounts. From there you can message, share
the profile link, add the person to a group, block or unblock, and report
the chat, also deleting it.
