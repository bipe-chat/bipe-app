**Send and preview files on Mac.** Choose a file from the attachment menu, drop it into a conversation or paste it from Finder. Add a caption or send it as a reply. Received files show their name and size, open in Quick Look, and can be saved with their extension visible and macOS quarantine. Temporary copies are removed after use.
