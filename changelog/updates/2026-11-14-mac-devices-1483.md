**The Mac shows every device on your account, and your phone shows the Mac by name.** Aparelhos e assistentes on the Mac lists this Mac first as "Este aparelho", then each phone, computer, browser and assistant with its model, system, app, last use and place. Click a device to see when it was linked and its ID. The Mac sends its model, macOS version and Bipe version to your other devices, so your phone lists it as "MacBook Air · macOS 26.1" and not as a bare name. To sign a device out, use Bipe on your phone.
