**Sign-up looks like Bipe on every step.** Each step has an açaí header, a progress bar and a large filled button at the bottom. Fields have a frame that lights up when you type, the PIN has one box per digit, and paste and scan are real buttons. Every PIN field now gives a light tap for each digit, a buzz for a wrong PIN and a success tap when the PIN is set. The Mac sign-in screen shows the same açaí cover next to the QR code.
