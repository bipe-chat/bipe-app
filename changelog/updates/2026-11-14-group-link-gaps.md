**Group links survive sign-up and sign-in, get a copy button and a QR code, work on the Mac, and stop at 256 people.** A group link that came before the account now opens the group after sign-up or after you sign back in, and a failed join after sign-up gets a second try. On iPhone, the welcome screen offers to paste the link that the bipe.chat page copied before the App Store opened. The link screen now copies the link and shows its QR code. On the Mac, admins make and manage the link from the group chat toolbar, and a group link in a message opens the group. A group with 256 members says it is full and takes nobody new.
