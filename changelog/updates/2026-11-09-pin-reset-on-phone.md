**Forgot your PIN? Reset it on your phone.** On a signed-in phone with Face ID or a fingerprint, "Esqueci minha senha" lets you set a new 8-digit Bipe PIN without a recovery email. It starts working after a safety wait, your other devices get an alert and can cancel, and then the app offers to add a recovery email.
