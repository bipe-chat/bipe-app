**Work accounts with more than one person can divide their chats.** When several people answer for one work account, Conversas has the filters Comigo, Sem ninguém and Todas, and each chat shows who has it. Take a chat, pass it to someone else, or put it back so anyone can answer. Notes on a chat stay inside the account. Your colleagues see who sent each reply, and customers still see only the account.
