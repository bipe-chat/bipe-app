**Mac: search finds messages, in every chat or in one chat.** The sidebar
search lists matching messages under the chats, and a click opens the chat
at that message. The search button in a chat searches only that chat.
Recent searches show while the field is empty. The Mac indexes the
messages it receives after this update; older history is not indexed.
