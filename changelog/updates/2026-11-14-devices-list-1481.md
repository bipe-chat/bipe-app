**Every device on your account shows what it is, and you sign it out from the list.** Aparelhos e assistentes lists this phone first as "Este aparelho", then each phone, computer, browser and assistant with its model, system, app, last use and place. A device opens its own page with the date it was linked and its ID. Desconectar works from the row or the page after Face ID or your fingerprint, with no web page, and "Desconectar todos os outros" signs out every other device at once.
